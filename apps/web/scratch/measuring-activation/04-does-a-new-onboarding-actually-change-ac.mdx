title: "Does a New Onboarding Actually Change Activation"
order: 4
---

Fernway tested a "guided setup" onboarding flow against the existing one, splitting a later signup week into two parallel groups.

| Group | Signups | Activated | Activation rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old onboarding | 260 | 118 | 45.4% |
| Guided setup (new) | 255 | 153 | 60% |

![Bar chart comparing activation rate between Fernway's old onboarding and a new guided-setup onboarding](https://syhpher-next-datastore-gvaf.b-cdn.net/svgs/measuring-activation/onboarding-ab-test.svg)

*Shown as a bar chart because this is a direct comparison between two onboarding variants, not a trend.*

The guided setup lifted activation from 45.4% to 60%, a gain of 14.6 percentage points, on comparably sized groups (260 vs 255 signups). That is large enough, and the groups similar enough in size, to act on rather than dismiss as noise.

Fernway rolled the guided setup out to all new signups the following week. The decision came directly from this comparison, not from which onboarding "felt" more polished.

**TRY IT**

**1. Group A converted 118 of 260. If it had instead converted 118 of 130 (half the sample size, same activated count), would you trust the resulting rate as much?**

**Suggested answer:** No, even though the rate would look higher (90.8%), a sample of 130 is much smaller and more easily skewed by a handful of unusually engaged users. Trusting an A/B result also depends on the group sizes being large enough and comparable, not just on the headline rate.

**2. What is one change other than onboarding that could produce a fake-looking lift in Group B's activation rate, and how would you rule it out?**

**Suggested answer:** If Group B happened to be recruited through a different, more highly-intentioned channel (say, an existing user's referral list) instead of a random split of the same signup pool, its higher activation could be about who joined, not about the onboarding flow. Ruling it out means confirming both groups were randomly assigned from the same signup source in the same week.
