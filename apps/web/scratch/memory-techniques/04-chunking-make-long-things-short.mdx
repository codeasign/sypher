Try to hold this string of digits in your head for ten seconds: 0 7 9 1 2 3 5 6 4 8 2 1.

Now try again with the numbers grouped: 0791 235 648 21. The second is much easier, though it has the same digits. The only difference is that you grouped them.

## What chunking is

A chunk is a group of items that your mind treats as one. Because short-term memory holds only a few items at a time, turning many small items into a few chunks lets you hold more.

Phone numbers are already chunked for this reason. So are postcodes, card numbers on a bank card, and dates.

## Find natural groups

Look for patterns in the material you need to learn:

* Numbers can be split into groups of three or four.
* A shopping list can be grouped by section: fruit, dairy, cleaning.
* A speech can be grouped by the three main points.
* A password can become a short sentence.

## Give each chunk a label

Chunks are easier if each has a name. Instead of remembering twelve steps of a process, remember "prepare, cook, serve", with a few steps under each.

Bad version: "I need to remember eighteen things for the trip."

Better version: "Three groups: documents, clothes, electronics. Documents means passport, tickets, insurance." The three labels are what you memorize first.

This is better because you hold three heads, and each head pulls up its details.

## Chunk what you study

Break a chapter into sections of five to seven ideas. Learn one chunk, review it, and then move to the next.

## Use meaning as glue

Random items are hardest. Items that connect by meaning, such as "morning routine", are easiest. When you can, group things by meaning instead of by order.

## TRY IT

Exercise 1. Chunk this number for easier recall: 4155550198.

**Suggested answer:** "415 555 0198." Three chunks, like a phone number.

Exercise 2. Group this list into three categories: bread, shampoo, milk, soap, eggs, towels, cheese, toothpaste, butter.

**Suggested answer:** "Food: bread, milk, eggs, cheese, butter. Bathroom: shampoo, soap, toothpaste. Household: towels." Now you hold three labels.

Exercise 3. You have to remember six steps to set up a new laptop. Write two chunk labels.

**Suggested answer:** "Set up: sign in, connect Wi-Fi, update. Then install: email, browser, work tools."
