Numbers are among the hardest things to remember, because they have no shape. The digit 7 is not a picture, and neither is 4719.

The trick is to give numbers a shape.

## Make numbers into pictures

Give each digit a simple image based on how it looks or sounds:

* 0 is a ball or a wheel
* 1 is a candle or a pencil
* 2 is a swan
* 3 is a heart on its side
* 4 is a sailboat
* 5 is a hook
* 6 is a snail
* 7 is a cliff
* 8 is a snowman
* 9 is a balloon on a string

Now the number 4719 becomes a sailboat, a cliff, a candle, and a balloon. Put them in a small scene: a sailboat crashes into a cliff, a candle burns on top of it, and a balloon carries the whole thing away.

## Use existing meaning

Look for dates, ages, and other numbers you know. 1492 is the year Columbus sailed. A door code that matches a birthday is easy to recall (but do not use it for security).

## Combine with places

For a long list, place items along a memory palace path, as in lesson 6. For a long number, cut it into chunks, make a picture for each chunk, and place each picture at one spot.

## Stay practical

You do not need to memorize your bank card. Use this for things you need to hold in your head: a code you are given by phone, a room number, a set of times.

Bad version: "I will remember the code, it is 8362."

Better version: "8362: snowman, heart, snail, swan. A snowman gives a heart to a snail on a swan."

This is better because it turned four bare digits into one scene.

## Write down what matters

Techniques are for what you cannot write down. For anything important, also write it in a safe place.

## TRY IT

Exercise 1. Turn the number 2058 into a picture story using the list above.

**Suggested answer:** "A swan wearing a ball on its head holds a hook and a snowman." Any consistent story works.

Exercise 2. Write a way to remember a meeting on the 14th at 3:30.

**Suggested answer:** "A candle and a sailboat on the 14th, a heart on a clock at 3:30." Or write the date in your calendar and use a short picture as backup.

Exercise 3. Chunk and picture the number 358 7141.

**Suggested answer:** "358: heart, hook, snowman. 7141: cliff, candle, sailboat, candle." Place both parts in a story.
