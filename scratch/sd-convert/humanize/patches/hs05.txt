<<<<
Every team at Netflix, Uber, and Discord has a story of a cascading failure that was stopped by a circuit breaker. The one team that did not implement circuit breakers caused a production outage. The cost of implementing circuit breakers (a few hours per service) is dwarfed by the cost of a single P0 outage.
====
Large-scale operators such as Netflix built their resilience tooling because cascading failures kept happening. Adding a circuit breaker, timeout, and fallback to each dependency call takes a few hours per service, which is dwarfed by the cost of a single major outage.
>>>>
<<<<
Even Amazon's own retail site uses scheduled scaling for Prime Day, not reactive auto-scaling.

Retailers typically pre-scale capacity for known events such as Prime Day or Black Friday rather than relying on reactive auto-scaling alone.
