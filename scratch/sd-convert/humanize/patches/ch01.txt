<<<<
Jump hash assigns exactly `1/N` of the keyspace to each bucket; no weighting is possible.
====
Jump hash assigns exactly `1/N` of the keyspace to each bucket; no weighting is possible. It also numbers buckets 0 to N-1, so you can only grow or shrink the cluster at the end: you cannot remove an arbitrary node from the middle without renumbering.
