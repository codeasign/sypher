At small scale (under 10 nodes), the overhead is negligible. At large scale (hundreds of nodes), coordination, monitoring, and replication traffic take a noticeable share of total resources, often in the range of 5-15%. At hyperscale (thousands of nodes), that share can grow much larger without careful engineering. These percentages are rules of thumb, not measurements from a specific system.

====
2. **Strong consistency is legally mandated**: Financial systems that require ACID transactions across all data. Horizontal scaling does not have to mean eventual consistency (Spanner and CockroachDB are strongly consistent), but strongly consistent distributed transactions add latency and operational complexity, and simpler eventually consistent designs may not meet compliance requirements.
