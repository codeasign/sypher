- The health score is computed as a weighted average: 0.4 × (1 - CPU utilization) + 0.3 × (1 - memory pressure) + 0.2 × (1 - disk latency percentile) + 0.1 × (1 - queue depth ratio).
- **Caveat:** This design has no safety guarantee on its own. Nodes may see different scores because of message delays or partitions, so two nodes could each believe they are the healthiest, and a partition could produce two leaders. A safer design keeps a quorum-based protocol such as Raft for the actual election and uses health only as a preference, for example by having the leader transfer leadership to the healthiest follower, or by giving unhealthy nodes a lower priority.
