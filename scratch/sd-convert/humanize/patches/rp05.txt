Every major outage in replication systems (GitHub's 2018 MySQL failover that took 43 seconds but felt like an eternity, Slack's 2020 PostgreSQL failover that caused 30 minutes of degraded performance) occurred during or immediately after a failover. Test failover in staging every week, not once a year.
====
Many of the worst replication incidents happen during or right after a failover. GitHub's October 2018 incident began with a 43-second network partition, triggered an automated failover to another data center, and took more than 24 hours to fully recover because writes existed only on one side. Test failover in staging regularly, not once a year, and include short partitions in your tests.
>>>>
