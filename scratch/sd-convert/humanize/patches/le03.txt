A single global leader would be too slow for most applications. There are two common solutions. One is to accept per-region leaders with asynchronous cross-region replication (effectively a multi-leader design, with the consistency limits described below). The other is to split the data into many partitions and place each partition's leader near the users who write to it, as Spanner and CockroachDB do, so each partition stays strongly consistent and only cross-partition operations pay the cross-region cost.
