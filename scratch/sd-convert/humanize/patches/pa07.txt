**PACELC classification:** In the Else regime, this is EL with a bounded-staleness guarantee (read-your-writes within 500ms), which sits between pure EL and pure EC. In the Partition regime, requiring a majority for writes is a consistency-first choice, while still serving reads on both sides. So it is roughly PC/EL with a bounded-staleness twist, not a clean member of one of the four standard quadrants.
