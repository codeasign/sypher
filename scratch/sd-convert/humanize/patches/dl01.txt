The leader keeps sending entries to Node 3 in the background, but that does not delay the commit. The slow node can matter in two indirect ways: if it falls far behind, the leader has to buffer or resend more entries for it, and if it later becomes leader or is needed for a quorum after another failure, its slowness suddenly counts. In a 3-node cluster the picture is the same as long as the other two nodes are healthy: the leader plus one fast follower is a majority, so commits stay fast. Latency only degrades when a second node is slow or down, because then the slow node's acknowledgement is needed.
