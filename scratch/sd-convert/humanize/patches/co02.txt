The trade-off: the last entry in the batch is not visible until the batch is flushed. A node must not acknowledge an entry, or count it toward a quorum, until that entry is durable on its disk, otherwise a crash could lose an entry that the cluster believes is committed.

In Raft-based systems, this can be achieved through leadership transfer: the current leader hands off to a preferred follower, which then runs an immediate election.
