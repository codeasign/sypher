====
For two-data-center deployments, use 3 nodes in the primary data center and 2 in the secondary (total 5), ensuring the primary can operate in isolation if the secondary goes down. The catch is that losing the primary data center then loses the quorum, so a third location (even a single small witness node) is better if you need to survive either site failing.
