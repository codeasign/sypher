**A distributed alternative (escrow)**: To keep working through partitions, give each pick station a lease on a slice of each SKU's stock (an escrow or "bounded counter"). The station decrements its own slice locally, with no coordination, and requests a new slice from the central tracker when it runs low. The sum of all slices never exceeds the real quantity, so overselling is impossible, and the cost is that unused stock can sit idle in a slice.
