At 10K writes/second, replication lag is typically 50ms. At 100K writes/second, lag grows to 500ms or more. At 500K writes/second, the async replication pipeline saturates and lag can reach seconds to minutes. Reads increasingly return stale data.
====
As write volume grows, replication lag grows with it. A lightly loaded cluster may see tens of milliseconds of lag, a heavily loaded one can see hundreds of milliseconds, and once the asynchronous replication pipeline saturates, lag can reach seconds or more. Reads increasingly return stale data. (The exact numbers depend on hardware and workload.)

<<<<
All writes go through the active region and are replicated synchronously to the passive region (or asynchronously, but reads from passive are guaranteed to see all data from active within bounded staleness).
====
All writes go through the active region. If they are replicated synchronously to the passive region, reads from the passive region are consistent, at the cost of cross-region write latency. If they are replicated asynchronously, writes are fast but reads from the passive region can be stale by the replication lag.

<<<<
- Each write requires prepare + commit: 2x message amplification
- Quorum requires 2 of 3 nodes: 2/3 fraction
- Total amplification: 3 * 2 * (2/3) = 4x
- Effective throughput needed: 400K ops/s
====
- Each write is replicated to 3 replicas and needs two coordination rounds (prepare + commit)
- Assume this costs roughly 4x the work of a simple PA/EL write (a rough planning figure)
- Effective throughput needed: 400K ops/s

<<<<
For 1M writes/s at 1KB per record, 3 regions: 1M * 1KB * 2 (replicas in other regions) = 2GB/s per region pair. This requires dedicated cross-region links.

For PC/EC sync replication: bandwidth = write_throughput * (2 round trips) * average_record_size * replicas_per_region. Same 1M writes pattern: 2GB/s * 2 = 4GB/s per region pair.
====
For 1M writes/s at 1KB per record, 3 regions, with each region's writes copied to the 2 other regions: about 2GB/s of outbound cross-region traffic per region (1GB/s to each of the other two). This requires dedicated cross-region links.

For PC/EC sync replication, the volume of data is about the same, because the extra round trips carry small acknowledgments and coordination messages. The cost of synchronous replication shows up as latency and as the cost of waiting, not as much extra bandwidth.

**Partition detected (PC/EC):** Alert immediately. Minority-side clients are seeing errors. Force failover if the partition does not heal within 30 seconds.
====
**Partition detected (PC/EC):** Alert immediately. Minority-side clients are seeing errors. The majority side keeps serving automatically. Do not manually force the minority side to accept writes, since that is what causes split-brain. Reroute clients to the majority side if the partition lasts.
