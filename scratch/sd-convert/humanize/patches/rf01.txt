Each receiving node compares its own log with the candidate's log. A node grants its vote only if: (a) the candidate's term is at least as large as the node's current term, and (b) the candidate's log is at least as up-to-date as the node's log (last term is higher, or last term equal and log at least as long).

Each receiving node compares its own log with the candidate's log. A node grants its vote only if: (a) the candidate's term is at least as large as the node's current term, (b) the node has not already voted for a different candidate in this term, and (c) the candidate's log is at least as up-to-date as the node's log (last term is higher, or last term equal and log at least as long).

When a majority of nodes (including the leader) have stored the entry, the leader marks the entry as committed at that index. It then applies the entry to its state machine. (A subtle rule: a leader commits an entry from an earlier term only indirectly, by committing a newer entry from its own term that follows it. Committing by counting replicas is limited to entries from the leader's current term.)
