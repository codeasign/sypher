Cassandra performs read repair probabilistically: you can configure the chance of performing a full read repair on each read (default is 10 percent). Full read repair on every read is too expensive. Probabilistic repair balances consistency and performance.
====
Older Cassandra versions performed background read repair probabilistically: a table setting controlled the chance (0.1 by default) that a read would compare all replicas. Cassandra 4.0 removed that setting. Now, when the replicas that answered a quorum read disagree, the coordinator repairs them as part of that read. Repairing on every read of every replica would be too expensive, which is why the background version was probabilistic.
