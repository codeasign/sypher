5. **Clock size**: With 100 editors, each vector clock has 100 entries. Pruning is not effective because all editors modify the document. Use hierarchical clocks: group editors by session and use a per-session counter.

6. **Garbage collection**: Editors that disconnect for more than 5 minutes are removed from active tracking. Their operations are considered complete and no longer need individual tracking.
====
5. **Clock size**: With 100 editors, the clock has up to 100 entries (about 1.6 KB), which is small. The real cost is attaching a full clock to every small edit, so real systems identify each operation by (client ID, counter) and exchange state vectors only when two replicas synchronize.

6. **Garbage collection**: Entries for editors who have left are tiny, so the simplest policy is to keep them. If they must be removed, compact them only after every replica has acknowledged the editor's operations, so that no replica still needs them to order edits.
