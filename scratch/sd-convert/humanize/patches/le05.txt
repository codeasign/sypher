In a 2-node replica set, adding an arbiter creates a 3-vote system where 2 votes are needed for majority. This allows a 2-node + arbiter deployment to survive a single node failure. The arbiter is lightweight (runs on a small VM) and requires no data storage. The catch is that an arbiter holds no data, so with one data node down, majority write concern cannot be satisfied by the arbiter, and MongoDB generally recommends a third data-bearing member instead.
