30 percent yearly growth: each shard will grow from 250 GB to 325 GB in year 2, to 422 GB in year 3.

Rebalancing trigger: when the largest shard exceeds 80 percent of the target per-shard storage (400 GB for a 500 GB disk), add shards.
====
With 30 percent yearly growth, the yearly write volume compounds: 45.6 TB, then 59.3 TB, then 77.1 TB. The cumulative total after 3 years is about 182 TB, or about 237 TB with indexes. Spread across 1024 shards, that is about 231 GB per shard, close to the 250 GB target.

Rebalancing trigger: when the largest shard exceeds 80 percent of the disk (400 GB for a 500 GB disk), add shards.
