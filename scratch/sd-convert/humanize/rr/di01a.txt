Your e-commerce platform just processed its millionth order, and the operations team is celebrating. Then a support engineer notices something wrong. After you sharded the orders database by region, each shard kept its own auto-increment counter, so order 982531 exists in the US shard and a completely different order 982531 exists in the EU shard. A customer quotes "order 982531" in a support ticket, and nobody can tell which one they mean. You could fix this by going back to one central counter, but then every new order across all regions must wait its turn for the next number, and at peak traffic your order service spends more time waiting for an ID than processing the order.
