## Netflix Dynomite

Netflix's Dynomite is a Dynamo-inspired layer that sits in front of storage engines such as Redis and Memcached and adds replication across nodes and data centers. It borrows Dynamo's ring topology, replication and tunable consistency, but as far as its public documentation shows it does not track vector clocks: it leaves conflict handling to the underlying engine, which in practice means last-write-wins behavior. It is a useful reminder that "Dynamo-inspired" does not imply "uses vector clocks", and that many production systems choose simpler conflict handling when their data allows it.
