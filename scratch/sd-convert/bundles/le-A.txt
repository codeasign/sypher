  N2->>N4: ELECTION

  N4->>N3: T=5 OK (Node 4 responds)
  Note over N3: T=6 backs off (Node 3 yields)
  Note over N4: T=7 No higher IDs for Node 4 - Node 4 wins
  N4->>N3: T=8 COORDINATOR (Node 4 sends COORDINATOR)
  N4->>N2: COORDINATOR
  N4->>N1: COORDINATOR
  Note over N4: Steady state begins
  N4->>N1: Heartbeat
  N4->>N2: Heartbeat
  N4->>N3: Heartbeat
  Note over N1,N4: Message types:<br/>ELECTION - I think the leader is dead, who should take over?<br/>OK - I am alive and have a higher ID, back off<br/>COORDINATOR - I am the new coordinator, acknowledge me<br/>Heartbeat - I am still alive and coordinating
