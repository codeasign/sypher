    @L s3 STAGE 3: QUORUM VOTE;;Leader appends entry to its Raft log;;Sends AppendEntries RPC to followers;;Waits for majority (2/3 or 3/5) to acknowledge;;If majority acknowledges → entry is committed;;If quorum not reached within timeout → FAILURE

    @L s5 STAGE 5: APPLY TO REGISTRY;;Lock registry applies the committed log entry:;;Insert: resource=res_A, owner=svr_7, token=1042,;;ttl=30000, expires=NOW+30000, status=HELD;;Verify: resource was FREE (reject if already HELD)
