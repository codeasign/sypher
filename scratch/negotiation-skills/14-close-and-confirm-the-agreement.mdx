title: "Close and Confirm the Agreement"
order: 14
---

People often relax as soon as they hear, "That works." Then they leave with different memories of the price, scope, date, or next step. Closing is the work of turning verbal agreement into shared terms.

## Recap before the conversation ends

Say the agreement in plain language and ask the other person to confirm it.

> "We agreed on $3,500, half paid upfront, delivery by May 12, and two revision rounds. You'll send the contract today, and I'll start after the deposit arrives. Did I miss anything?"

Include the terms that would cause trouble if remembered differently: money, dates, quantities, responsibilities, conditions, and what happens next.

## Put important terms in writing

For a casual household trade, a message may be enough. For work, housing, services, or a large purchase, use the appropriate written agreement. A friendly conversation is not a substitute for clear terms.

Send the recap while the conversation is fresh:

> "Thanks for the call. My notes are: I will deliver the first draft Friday, you will return consolidated comments Monday, and the final payment is due on approval. Please reply if your understanding differs."

Writing is not an accusation. It gives both sides a chance to catch a misunderstanding before it costs more.

## Check conditions and authority

An agreement may depend on approval, inspection, financing, references, or another event. Name that condition and the deadline.

> "The offer is agreed subject to finance approval by Tuesday. If approval does not arrive, neither side is committed."

Also confirm that the person making the agreement can approve it. "I'll ask my manager" is a next step, not a final deal.

## Do what you agreed

Negotiation does not end when the document is signed. Send the deposit, introduce the contact, deliver the file, or schedule the review you promised. If something changes, raise it before the deadline rather than hoping the other person will not notice.

## PRACTICE

**1. Close a freelance agreement.** The agreed terms are $2,400, 40 percent upfront, delivery in three weeks, and two revisions. Write the recap.

**Suggested answer:** "To confirm, the fee is $2,400 with 40 percent due upfront, delivery three weeks after the deposit, and two revision rounds. I'll send the agreement today. Does that match your notes?"

**2. Name the condition.** A job offer depends on a reference check by Friday. Write the condition clearly.

**Suggested answer:** "We have agreed on the role and terms, subject to the reference check being completed by Friday. Please tell me when that condition is cleared."

**3. Correct a different memory.** The client replies that they remember three revisions, but you agreed on two. Write a calm response.

**Suggested answer:** "My notes show two revision rounds, which is also what the fee covers. If you need a third, I can add it for $300. Please confirm which option you want." The response corrects the term and offers a clear alternative.
