"Sign today or the offer disappears." A deadline may be real. It may also be an attempt to make you decide before you can compare, calculate, or ask for advice. You do not need to diagnose the other person's character. You need a response that protects your decision.

## Slow the pace

Pressure works by shrinking the time between hearing a demand and answering it. Put the time back.

> "I don't make this size of decision during the call. Send the terms in writing, and I'll respond by tomorrow at noon."

If they refuse a reasonable review period, that refusal is information about the agreement you would be entering.

## Test a claimed limit

The other person may say, "I have no authority," "This is company policy," or "Everyone accepts these terms." Do not call them a liar. Ask a question that makes the limit specific.

> "Which part is fixed by policy, and who can approve an exception?"

> "If price cannot move, which terms can you change?"

A real constraint becomes clearer. A vague tactic often becomes less powerful when it has to survive a precise question.

## Do not reward surprise changes

Some people add a demand after you have agreed on everything else. They rely on your desire to finish.

> "That term was not part of the agreement we reached. If it changes, I need to review the whole package again."

Return to the full agreement. Do not treat the new demand as a small final favor if it changes the value of the deal.

## Set a boundary on behavior

Personal insults, threats, repeated interruptions, or false information are not advanced negotiation moves. Name the behavior once and state what you will do.

> "I can discuss the price, but I won't continue while I'm being insulted. We can pause and try again tomorrow."

Leaving a conversation is different from abandoning the negotiation. A pause can protect both the decision and the relationship. If safety is a concern, leave and get appropriate support rather than trying to win the exchange.

## PRACTICE

**1. Respond to an exploding deadline.** A seller says, "This price is available only if you transfer the deposit in the next hour." Write your response.

**Suggested answer:** "I won't transfer a deposit without reviewing the written terms. If the price cannot stay open until tomorrow, I'll pass." The response protects your process and accepts that walking away may be necessary.

**2. Test authority.** A recruiter says, "HR does not negotiate starting salary." Ask two questions.

**Suggested answer:** "Is the salary band fixed, or only this initial offer? If salary cannot change, who can approve a signing payment or an earlier review?" The questions test the limit and open other terms.

**3. Stop a late demand.** After agreeing on price and delivery, a client adds unlimited revisions. Write one sentence.

**Suggested answer:** "Unlimited revisions change the scope we priced, so we need either a revision limit or a new fee before we confirm." The new demand returns to the full package.
